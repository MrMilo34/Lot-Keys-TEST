FAST SETUP

1) WEBSITE — PC
   Open your MrMilo34 / Lot-Keys-TEST repository and the newest successful Actions run.
   Download LotKeys-Phone-Mirror-Source for the complete repository folder backup.
   The main branch deploys through GitHub Pages; do not upload a partial patch or nested folder.
   Reload LotKeys on PC and phone and confirm version 0.9.4.95.
   Do NOT clear site storage, delete customer data, reset the app, or replace working pairing files.
   Hard-refresh the desktop if necessary; close/reopen the phone PWA after deployment.

2) ANDROID — YOUR PHONE
   Stop/disable the OLD LotKeys Device Bridge notification app first.
   Install LotKeys-Phone-Mirror-0.2.0-Test.apk.
   It installs separately as "LotKeys Phone Mirror (Test)".
   You do NOT need to change your default messaging app or create phone contacts.
   Open it and press "1. Allow SMS access". Read and approve the SMS permission request.
   Press "2. Allow background connection" and approve the system prompt.
   Keep this test companion out of sleeping/deep-sleeping apps in your phone settings.
   Paste the SAME device.json used with your existing relay (role MUST be "device").
   Press "Save pairing & start mirror". A persistent mirroring notification should appear.
   Do not paste device.json into the LotKeys WEBSITE.

3) PC AND PHONE LOTKEYS
   Keep your existing Python relay and HTTPS tunnel windows running.
   In BOTH LotKeys browser instances, use the SAME hub.json pairing (role "hub").
   Existing saved pairing can be unlocked with its PIN rather than re-pasted.
   Pairing credentials are not shared through customer Drive storage.
   Open Hub > All or Device. Clear the search field.
   The banner must identify Phone Mirror and say "SMS/MMS history".
   "Android notification bridge [prototype]" means the old companion is still responding.
   "RCS not included" is intentional; a private RCS-only thread will not appear in this build.

FIRST LIVE TEST (USE A WILLING TEST RECIPIENT, NOT A CUSTOMER)

A. Find a conversation known to contain ordinary SMS. Verify older phone messages appear.
B. Open it on both PC and phone LotKeys. Confirm the same history is present.
C. Use Send SMS on PC. Verify it arrives and appears in your normal phone messaging app.
D. Reply directly from the normal phone messaging app using SMS. Verify both LotKeys views update.
E. Turn the phone screen OFF for two minutes. Verify mirroring still updates from another device.
F. Disconnect internet, try a draft, and confirm there is no delayed automatic send.
   Restore internet. Verify automatic reconnection and refreshed history.
G. Turn the phone OFF. Allow about 25 seconds for heartbeat expiry. History should hide and
   sending should disable; saved customers/notes/documents/appointments should remain.
H. Restart/unlock the phone and check automatic recovery without manually reopening the companion.
   This is a required device acceptance check, not something the automated tests proved.

SMS PERMISSION DOES NOT SHOW "ALLOWED"?

Stop and diagnose permission access before judging synchronization.
Android treats SMS permissions as restricted; some installation paths may not grant access.
The source/test kit includes an OPTIONAL INSTALL-WITH-ADB.cmd for your own authorized phone.
It uses the standard Android developer install flow; it does not switch your SMS app, root the
phone, disable security protection, or force an SMS. Read its consent prompt before running.
If policy or the installer still denies SMS access, keep using the phone and report the status.
Do not enable a hidden/accessibility workaround or disable protection just to force installation.

SENDING SAFETY

Send SMS is SMS, not RCS. Carrier charges may apply.
"Sent by phone" does not prove recipient delivery.
If the result is "Not confirmed", check the normal messaging app before retrying.
Cancel draft removes the local draft; it cannot recall a message already handed to the phone.
There is no automatic outbox that resends later when the connection returns.
Group threads and short codes are view-only. Use your normal phone app for those.

WHAT THIS BUILD DOES NOT DO

No private RCS history or RCS sending. No MMS sending or attachment downloads.
MMS text can be shown; media is labelled "open on phone".
No new-number compose flow; reply to existing supported SMS threads only.
No replacement hosted relay: the existing relay/tunnel must stay up.
A changed temporary tunnel address still needs updated pairing.
This is an Android test APK, not an iPhone companion or a Google Play release.

PRESERVED

Phone communication stays on the phone. The mirror uses transient in-memory views.
The Hub CRM store, internal messaging module, processor, extension and artwork are unchanged.
Do not manually delete or reset them during installation.
