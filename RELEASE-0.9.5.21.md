# LotKeys TEST V0.9.5.21 — chat layout, type and page headers

## Changes

- Full LotKeys Chat history and composer expand across the available conversation area, with clearance for the PC action rail. Message bubbles can reach 620px, similar to the Device chat.
- Incoming LotKeys messages use the same light white and dark card surface as incoming Device messages, with a subtle border.
- Full Device SMS/MMS message bodies now use the in-app chat's 14px size and 1.35 line spacing. Both chats already share the app font family.
- Home, Inventory, Listings, Garage and Account top headers become white in light theme and black in dark theme.

## Compatibility and test limits

This web-only TEST update builds on V0.9.5.20. The V0.9.5.12 Android connector and pairing remain unchanged. Compare the layouts on a paired phone and PC during team testing.
