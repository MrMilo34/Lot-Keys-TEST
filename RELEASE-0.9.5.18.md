# LotKeys V0.9.5.18 — contact header and trusted computer names

## What changed

- Device conversation headers show up to two lines of the customer's name above the phone number. The redundant **SMS/MMS live** line is removed from that compact card, leaving room for a name such as Gagandeep Singh beside the Interested Vehicle summary.
- On the phone, **Phone connection → 🖥️ Connected** now offers **Rename** for each trusted computer. The chosen label appears in that list and in the active connection display. Leaving the name blank restores the original browser label. Forgetting a computer removes its label.
- The V0.9.5.17 order and styling remain: **Notes, Questions, Call, Media, Booking, Organize** with the high-contrast Organize button in both themes.

Computer nicknames are saved in this phone's LotKeys browser. They do not rename the Android system notification or sync to a different phone/browser.

## Compatibility

This is a web-only update. Keep the signed V0.9.5.12 Android connector installed. No APK reinstall, permission change, account re-selection, or re-pairing is required.
