Request LotKeys account deletion

For this controlled LotKeys pilot, the LotKeys Creator handles deletion requests manually. Send your request from the Google email used for your LotKeys account so ownership can be verified.

Request account and personal-data deletion

You can also email BlairK34@Live.com with the subject “LotKeys account deletion request.” Include your account email and Store name. Do not include passwords, message contents or customer documents.

The request covers your LotKeys account/profile, personal Hub records, Listings and personal assets controlled by that account, and the account's phone/PC trust. Opening this page or drafting the email does not delete anything. You will receive instructions for removing local copies and revoking Google access after the request is reviewed.

Official dealership Inventory, approved contributions and messages retained by other participants have separate owners. Their deletion or retention needs that owner's review; a personal-account deletion cannot silently delete shared dealership records. Copies you downloaded, exported or sent outside LotKeys are outside this request.

Local browser data and retained Android upload files stay on your device until deliberately removed. Complete or review pending saves before removing local copies. Removing the Android application does not remove records stored in Google Drive.

This manual pilot process is not a claim of Google Play approval. Public release still requires a verified deletion workflow and a published policy stating any required retention and deletion timeframes.
